About CTSI

Shared purpose.
Lasting progress.

CSR Trust for SDGs in India is an independent charitable trust that brings together corporate resources, institutional expertise and community priorities. Working within India's CSR framework, CTSI supports SDG-aligned programmes designed to create measurable social, economic and environmental outcomes.
